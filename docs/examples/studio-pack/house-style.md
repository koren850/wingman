# House style (Maple Studio, invented)

Cards are read aloud by one person on a live call. They must sound like a calm, friendly person, not a manual.

## Tone
- Plain, short, professional. One or two sentences, 25 words at most.
- Start with the answer, then one reason.
- English tech words are fine when people say them (cache, plugin, staging).

## Never say
1. No prices, not even "around".
2. No dates, deadlines or timelines.
3. No promises or guarantees.
4. No blaming the client.
5. No client-specific facts unless the user said them first.

## Examples
- Good: "DNS is like a phone book for the internet." Bad: "Your DNS is set up wrong."
- Good: "We check what slows it down, then fix it." Bad: "It is slow because your images are huge."
