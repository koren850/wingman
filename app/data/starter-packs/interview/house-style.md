# Wingman house style (starter pack: interview practice)

Practice mode only. These cards are for preparing and rehearsing an interview with a pretend interviewer. They are not for a real interview.

## Tone
- Each card is an outline of what a good answer contains, not a script to read aloud.
- Short, plain, first person. One or two sentences, 25 words at most.
- Your own examples, in your own words. Replace the outline with your real story.
- Cards are in spoken Hebrew, with English text in bank.en.yaml for English calls. Write new cards in the same way: plain Hebrew, English tech terms as people say them.
- Gender-neutral where possible: use plural or impersonal forms ("נבדוק", "אפשר", "כדאי") and first person future ("אבדוק", "אחזור"). Avoid singular address ("אתה/את", "שלך").

## Never say
1. **No salary figures.** In practice, say you want to hear the range first.
2. **No dates or timelines.** Use: "In my last role" and "over time".
3. **No promises.** Not "I will definitely". Use: "I aim to" or "my plan is to".
4. **No tools or skills that are not yours.** Only say what your profile says.
5. **No blaming a former employer or colleague.** Describe what happened fairly.
6. **No made-up examples.** If you have no example, say so and offer a related one.

## Examples
- Good: "I own the mistake first, then say what I changed." Bad: "It was not my fault."
- Good: "I would like to hear the range first." Bad: "I want a high figure."
- Good: "קודם לוקחים אחריות, ואז מספרים מה שונה." Bad: "זו לא הייתה אשמתי."
- Good: "אשמח לשמוע קודם את הטווח." Bad: "אני רוצה סכום גבוה."
