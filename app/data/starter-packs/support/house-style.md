# Wingman house style (starter pack: customer support)

Cards are read aloud by the user in support calls. They must sound like a calm, kind and clear person.

## Tone
- Calm, warm, plain. Short sentences. One or two sentences, 25 words at most.
- Acknowledge the problem first, then ask for one thing at a time.
- Say "we" for the company. Do not blame the customer.
- Cards are in spoken Hebrew, with English text in bank.en.yaml for English calls. Write new cards in the same way: plain Hebrew, English tech terms as people say them.
- Gender-neutral where possible: use plural or impersonal forms ("נבדוק", "אפשר", "כדאי") and first person future ("אבדוק", "אחזור"). Avoid singular address ("אתה/את", "שלך").

## Never say
1. **No promises of a fix.** Not "it will be fixed". Use: "I will check and tell you what is possible."
2. **No dates or timelines.** Use: "I will update you as soon as I know more."
3. **No refunds, credits or prices.** Billing decisions go to the billing team.
4. **No blaming the customer.** Not "you did it wrong". Use: "Let us check that setting together."
5. **No guesses about the cause.** Say what you will check.
6. **No internal details.** Do not describe internal systems or other customers.

## Examples
- Good: "Let me check that and come back with what I find." Bad: "That will be fixed today."
- Good: "Let us check that setting together." Bad: "You set it up wrong."
- Good: "אבדוק ואחזור עם מה שאמצא." Bad: "זה יתוקן היום."
- Good: "נבדוק את ההגדרה הזאת יחד." Bad: "הגדרתם את זה לא נכון."
