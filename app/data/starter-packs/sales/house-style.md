# Wingman house style (starter pack: sales)

Cards are read aloud by the user in live sales calls. They must sound like a calm, honest person, not a brochure.

## Tone
- Friendly, plain, professional. Never pushy. One or two sentences, 25 words at most.
- Start with the answer, then one reason. Ask one question back when it helps the call move.
- Say "we" for the company and "you" for the prospect.
- Cards are in spoken Hebrew, with English text in bank.en.yaml for English calls. Write new cards in the same way: plain Hebrew, English tech terms as people say them.
- Gender-neutral where possible: use plural or impersonal forms ("נבדוק", "אפשר", "כדאי") and first person future ("אבדוק", "אחזור"). Avoid singular address ("אתה/את", "שלך").

## Never say
1. **No prices or discounts.** Not even "around". Use: "The price depends on the details. I will send a clear quote."
2. **No dates, timelines or SLAs.** Use: "It depends, let me check and come back with an estimate."
3. **No promises or guarantees.** Not "it will definitely work". Use: "Let me check and come back with an answer."
4. **No claims about other products.** Do not say what a competitor cannot do.
5. **No made-up customers, numbers or features.** If a fact is not in the cards, say you will check.
6. **No jargon without a short explanation.**

## Examples
- Good: "It connects to the common email and calendar tools. I will confirm your exact list." Bad: "It works with everything."
- Good: "I will check and come back with a clear figure." Bad: "It is cheap, you will love the price."
- Good: "זה מתחבר לכלי המייל והיומן הנפוצים. אאשר את הרשימה שלכם." Bad: "זה עובד עם הכול."
- Good: "אבדוק ואחזור עם מספר מדויק." Bad: "זה זול, תאהבו את המחיר."
